# Portal Roadmap

## Phase 1: Import Portal

Purpose:

- Manage imports into foreign Meril subsidiaries.

Scope:

- Upload Commercial Invoice, Packing List, and AWB.
- Extract and validate import details.
- Generate import shipment candidate.
- Generate product and batch candidates.
- Track country-specific document requirements.
- Post goods receipt into destination warehouse.

## Phase 2: Sales Portal

Purpose:

- Manage sales from a Meril subsidiary to customers or another Meril entity.

Scope:

- Customer orders
- Stock availability checks
- Sales shipment requests
- Dispatches
- Customer delivery tracking
- Customer batch traceability

## Phase 3: Inventory Portal

Purpose:

- Monitor and control global stock.

Scope:

- Warehouse inventory
- Batch traceability
- Serial traceability
- FEFO
- Expiry alerts
- Physical counts
- Reconciliation
- Inventory valuation

## Phase 4: Unified Control Tower

Purpose:

- Combine Import, Sales, and Inventory into one global monitoring layer.

Scope:

- Global stock movement view
- Entity-to-entity transfers
- Country dashboards
- Customer dashboards
- Shipment delay intelligence
- Expiry risk intelligence
- AI copilot
